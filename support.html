Frequently Asked Questions

How does DeepSeek Usage obtain my usage data?

The application uses the authentication information you provide to access your DeepSeek account usage information directly from DeepSeek services.

Does DeepSeek Usage upload my data?

No. Authentication credentials, usage data, and settings remain on your device and are not transmitted to the developer.

What platforms are supported?

Currently DeepSeek Usage is available for macOS.

How can I report a bug?

Please send a detailed description, screenshots, and your macOS version to the support email above.
