Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

DeepSeek Usage respects your privacy and is designed to operate primarily on your local device.

Information Collection

DeepSeek Usage does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information with the developer or any third party.

Authentication Information

To access DeepSeek usage data, users may provide browser authentication cookies or other credentials associated with their DeepSeek account.

These credentials are stored locally on the user's device and are used solely for the purpose of retrieving usage statistics, balances, and expense information from DeepSeek.

The application does not upload, transmit, share, or disclose these credentials to the developer or any third party.

Local Data Storage

  • Authentication cookies or credentials provided by the user
  • Usage history
  • Application settings and preferences

This information remains on your device unless explicitly removed by the user.

Third-Party Services

DeepSeek Usage communicates directly with DeepSeek services to retrieve account usage information.

No usage data, authentication information, or personal information is transmitted to the developer.

Analytics and Tracking

DeepSeek Usage does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, telemetry systems, or user profiling.

Data Sharing

No personal information, authentication credentials, or usage data is shared with any third party.
